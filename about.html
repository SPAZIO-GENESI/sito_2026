About Spazio Genesi

Mission & Vision

Spazio Genesi is an association of students from the Accademia di Belle Arti that manages an exhibition space for contemporary art. We are committed to fostering experimental curatorial practices, providing emerging artists with exhibition opportunities, and creating a vibrant community around contemporary visual culture.

What We Do

Exhibitions

We organize quarterly exhibitions featuring emerging and established artists, with a focus on contemporary practices in painting, sculpture, installation, and mixed media. Each exhibition is accompanied by curatorial texts and educational programming.

Events & Programs

Beyond exhibitions, we host artist talks, workshops, studio visits, and panel discussions. These events create opportunities for direct engagement between artists, students, and the broader community.

Archive & Resources

We maintain a comprehensive archive of past exhibitions, catalogs, documentation, and educational materials. These resources are freely available to students, researchers, and the general public.

Community Engagement

We are deeply invested in our community, organizing collaborative projects, supporting emerging voices, and creating spaces for critical discourse around contemporary art.

Our Team

Spazio Genesi is run by a collective of dedicated students and young professionals committed to contemporary art. Our team brings diverse perspectives and expertise across curatorial practice, exhibition design, education, and community engagement.

Working as a student-led association allows us to maintain independence, take creative risks, and directly respond to the needs and interests of our community. We are always looking for passionate collaborators and supporters.
